Climate Advisories for Farmers

🌧️ Monsoon & Precipitation: Ensure adequate drainage channels are cleared around root-sensitive crops such as tomatoes and chillies to prevent root rot (Phytophthora).

🐛 Pest Forecast: High humidity conditions increase susceptibility to powdery mildew and aphid outbreaks. Recommend preventive sprays of 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE).

🌾 Harvesting Guidelines: Harvest pulses and grains during dry midday hours to ensure moisture content remains below 12% for safe storage.
