Our Features

A complete platform serving every stakeholder in the agricultural ecosystem.

👨‍🌾

Farmer Marketplace

List your crops, manage orders and receive payments directly from buyers.

  • Farmers can register and create profiles.
  • Upload crop details and images.
  • Set crop prices and available quantities.
  • Receive orders directly from buyers.
  • Track product sales and order status.
🛒

Buyer Services

Explore fresh produce, place orders and track deliveries with ease.

  • Browse available agricultural products.
  • View crop details and farmer information.
  • Place orders securely.
  • Track delivery status.
  • View previous purchase history.
🛵

Secure Delivery

Delivery agents can manage orders and verify deliveries using OTP.

  • Track orders and delivery status.
  • Generate delivery OTP.
  • Verify delivery using OTP.
  • Update delivery progress.
  • Improve delivery security.

Agricultural Advisory

Farmers and buyers can receive expert agricultural guidance.

  • Connect farmers with expert advisors.
  • Get soil-based recommendations.
  • Receive district-based crop guidance.
  • Access sustainable farming advice.
  • Improve crop productivity.
